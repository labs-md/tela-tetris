# Fabric planning portal

Mode: Operate. Spanish guided garment planning on a 150 cm roll. User requests a wizard that guides the journey one step at a time, retaining the professional light theme and LABS accents. Preserve game files and engine behavior.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One decision at a time, with a visible four-step route and a clear next action. Configuration and results appear only when relevant.

OWN-WORLD: Existing white and neutral surfaces, charcoal text, Neuton headings, Inter controls, LABS yellow for active progress and primary actions, orange for measured roll length.

STORY: Choose a garment, enter its S–XL quantities, review or add another garment, then watch the calculated layout and read the yards to buy. Start empty, preserve quantities when going back, and block invalid or empty progression.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact masthead, four numbered progress buttons, current task title and one focused white panel. Garment selection uses three large illustrated buttons. Subsequent screens show quantity controls, a review with material assumptions, then the measured arrangement and purchase summary. Back/Continue sit in a stable panel footer.

FORM: User-directed wizard conversion inside the established LABS system, code-led, no concept roll for this precise interaction correction. Completed steps can be revisited; editing invalidates stale estimates, and leaving an active calculation cancels it. Signature motion remains actual polygon arrangement on the final step.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
